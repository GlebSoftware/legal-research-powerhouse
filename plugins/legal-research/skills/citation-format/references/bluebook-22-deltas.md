# Bluebook 22d ed. deltas

Addition to the modified citation-format skill; not part of the DingDuff original.

The Indigo Book restates the Uniform System of Citation as of the Bluebook 21st edition. The Bluebook 22d edition (May 2025) changed the items below. Status: listed from the planning research in `docs/02-architecture.md` (ADR-7); confirm each against the 22d edition text before relying on it in graded work.

| Topic | Indigo Book / Bluebook 21st | Bluebook 22d |
|---|---|---|
| Consolidated quotation-cleanup parenthetical | "(cleaned up)" | "(citation modified)" |
| Citing output of generative AI | No rule | Rule 18.3 governs citation of AI output |
| Internet sources | URL | Archive links for internet sources |

When the document must conform to the Bluebook (law review, a professor's Bluebook requirement), apply this table over the main skill. When a Georgia court is the audience, `georgia-court-rules.md` controls reporter choice.
