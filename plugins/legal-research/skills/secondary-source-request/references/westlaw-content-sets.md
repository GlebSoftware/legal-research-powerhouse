Everything below restates those pages. Navigation names not listed here are written in a request as "confirm on screen".

## Reaching secondary sources (1)

Westlaw Precision home, Content types tab, Secondary Sources. The Secondary Sources page takes keywords in the search bar or navigation by type, by state, by topic, or by publication series. The sidebar holds tools including the full Secondary Sources Library. Reference Attorneys answer source-specific questions (live chat is linked from the page).

## Full Secondary Sources Library (2)

Content types tab, Secondary Sources, then "Full Secondary Sources Library" under Tools & Resources. Filters: Publication Series, Topic, Jurisdiction, Publication Type. The page names one Publication Type value, "Text and Treatises", and gives the example Antitrust topic plus Federal jurisdiction plus Text and Treatises. Typing a source's short name in the search bar suggests its Content Pages: "amjur" for American Jurisprudence; the same works for Witkin and CalCrim.

## Categories (3)

| Category, as named | Description given | Navigation given |
|---|---|---|
| Encyclopedias | Brief articles giving a general understanding of a wide range of law; state encyclopedias exist for New York, Texas, and California | "Search Encyclopedias", "View all Encyclopedias" |
| American Law Reports | Summaries analyzing federal and state case law on particular issues; a Table of Cases, Laws, and Rules; annotations point to other secondary sources | "American Law Reports" link |
| Texts & Treatises | Works of scholarly depth or practical insight | "Search Texts & Treatises" |
| Jury Instructions | Elements or explanations for matters in typical cases | Secondary Sources, Jury Instructions, Jurisdiction filter, search the cause of action, open the instruction, related notes, "View Full TOC" |
| Restatements of the Law | Overviews of basic doctrines in areas of American law | "Search Restatements"; a Restatement's table of contents exports through download, "Outline of Current View" under "What to Deliver" |
| Law Reviews & Journals | Articles giving deep background on legal issues | "Law Reviews & Journals" link |

## Named publications (3)

American Jurisprudence ("more than 400 topics"), Corpus Juris Secundum, Causes of Action (overviews of causes of action and defenses with checklists and sample pleadings), Federal Jury Practice & Instructions, Federal Practice & Procedure, Federal Civil Procedure Before Trial, Treatise on Constitutional Law, Dobbs' Law of Torts, Black's Law Dictionary (multi-word terms in quotation marks). Codes named: UCC Article 2, Uniform Commercial Code. Restatements named: Restatement of Torts, Restatement of Contracts, Restatement of Property.

## How requests use these names

- A category or publication named above may appear in a request as a navigation path, for example "Secondary Sources > Restatements of the Law" or "Secondary Sources > Encyclopedias > American Jurisprudence".
- Naming a publication as a navigation path asserts nothing about its contents. Section numbers, volume numbers, chapter titles, and authors of specific works appear only when a retrieved primary source names them (request-template.md). Everything else is "locate".
- Georgia-specific secondary sources: none is named in these pages. Requests use "Secondary Sources > by state > Georgia (confirm on screen)" and the Jurisdiction filter of the full library.
